# 2026R1Tempest - R1 Robot Mechanical Structure Overview

## 📌 总体介绍 (Overview)
2026赛季 R1 机器人（代号 Tempest）的绝大部分核心机构由队长 **刘志轩** 主导设计。整体设计涵盖了移动、对抗、吸取与辅助配合等多个维度，最后由机械组组长 **张丹艺** 完成了最终的修改、优化与整合。

The vast majority of the core mechanisms for the 2026 season R1 robot (Codename: Tempest) were designed by Team Captain **Liu Zhixuan**. The overall design encompasses multiple dimensions including mobility, confrontation, intake, and auxiliary cooperation. Finally, the final modifications, optimizations, and integration were completed by Mechanical Team Leader **Zhang Danyi**.

## ⚙️ 核心机构设计 (Core Mechanisms)

由队长 **刘志轩** 主要设计的机构包括：
The mechanisms primarily designed by Captain **Liu Zhixuan** include:

*   **舵轮轮组 (Swerve Drive Modules)**：提供高机动性的全向移动能力。
    High-mobility omnidirectional movement capabilities.
*   **完整底盘 (Complete Chassis)**：作为整车的基础承载平台，保障结构稳定性。
    Serves as the foundational platform for the robot, ensuring structural stability.
*   **夹取武器杆与辅助对接机构 (Weapon Shaft Gripping & Auxiliary Docking Mechanisms)**：用于武器杆的精准夹取以及与其他机器人的辅助对接。
    Used for precise gripping of the weapon shaft and auxiliary docking with other robots.
*   **吸取 KFS 的双臂系统 (Dual-Arm KFS Intake System)**：专门用于高效吸取 KFS 的执行机构。
    Executive mechanism specifically designed for the efficient intake of KFS.
*   **R2 承载平台 (Platform for R2)**：专为 R2 机器人放置第3层九宫格 KFS 设计的配合平台。
    A cooperative platform specifically designed for the R2 robot to place 3rd-layer tic-tac-toe KFS.
