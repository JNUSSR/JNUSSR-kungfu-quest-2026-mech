# 2026R2Zenith - R2 Robot Mechanical Structure Overview

## 📌 总体介绍 (Overview)
2026赛季 R2 机器人（代号 Zenith）的机械结构设计充分考虑了多功能性与协同作业能力。其结构主要由麦轮移动底盘、多级抬升系统以及专用的对抗机械臂组成。整车结构由机械组成员协同设计，并由机械组组长 **张丹艺** 完成最终整合。

The mechanical structure of the 2026 season R2 robot (Codename: Zenith) fully considers versatility and collaborative operation capabilities. Its structure mainly consists of a Mecanum wheel mobile chassis, a multi-stage lifting system, and dedicated confrontation robotic arms. The overall structure was collaboratively designed by members of the mechanical team and finally integrated by Mechanical Team Leader **Zhang Danyi**.

---

## ⚙️ 核心机构设计 (Core Mechanisms)

R2 机器人的主要结构包含以下部分：
The main structure of the R2 robot includes the following parts:

*   **麦轮轮组 (Mecanum Wheel Drive Modules)**：提供灵活的平面移动能力。
    Provide flexible planar movement capabilities.
*   **完整底盘 (Complete Chassis)**：构建稳固的机械基础。
    Builds a solid mechanical foundation.
*   **前抬升机构 (Front Lifting Mechanism)** & **后抬升机构 (Rear Lifting Mechanism)**：用于跨越障碍或调整整车姿态。
    Used for obstacle crossing or adjusting the robot's posture.
*   **双级抬升机械臂 (Dual-stage Lifting Robotic Arm)**：由机械组成员 **曾炽强** 设计，用于高处的作业与对抗。
    Designed by mechanical team member **Zeng Zhiqiang**, used for high-altitude operations and confrontation.
*   **武器头夹持机构 (Weapon Head Gripping Mechanism)**：由机械组成员 **杨树燊** 设计，用于精准夹取与控制武器头。
    Designed by mechanical team member **Yang Shushen**, used for precise gripping and control of the weapon head.
